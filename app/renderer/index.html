Natural voice

Checking…

A realistic voice (Kokoro) made on this computer; nothing is sent anywhere. Each team member's voice is chosen on the Team page.

Grades are Kokoro's quality ratings (A is the most natural).

Meeting recorder

Say "Ryan, record this meeting" (or "Sara, record this appointment"). Jarvis records your microphone and your computer's sound, and transcribes it on this computer.

Off: the video and pictures are only used to write the notes, then deleted, so they don't pile up (the notes and the text transcript stay). On: they stay in your Videos folder under Jarvis Meetings (about 14 MB a minute), even if you uninstall Jarvis. Always tell people when you record them.

You

Coding supervisor

With the Coding supervisor on (Tool Hub), say "Ryan, add a login page to my project". Claude Code works on the folder you pick, in the background; you get its questions with a recommendation, and updates.

Always asked, at every level: deleting files, git push, installing software, using the network, anything outside the project folder, and files with keys or passwords.

This adds two small entries (marked "jarvis-claude-hook") to your Claude Code settings; turning it off removes exactly those.

Setup & uninstall

Uninstalling removes Jarvis and everything it kept on this computer: settings, chats, memory, team folders, voices, saved sign-ins and the Claude Code hooks it added. Claude Code itself, your own projects and any meeting videos you chose to keep are not touched.
