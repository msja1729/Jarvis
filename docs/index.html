Jarvis: Your Own Team of AI Experts

A personal assistant, a project manager, a doctor and a finance advisor on your computer. You talk to them by name, just like talking to a person, and they get the work done.

Source code on GitHub

Why I built Jarvis

This version of Jarvis is my first step towards a goal: giving everyone their own team of experts. It already gives you a team you can talk to, who remember you and work for you; I will keep making them more capable and more natural, until having your own team of experts feels as real as having the people themselves.

In real life, some people have a personal assistant who manages their day, a family doctor they can call any time, an advisor who looks after their money and a manager who keeps their projects on track. Most people have none of these: they are expensive, and they are not available whenever you need them.

Jarvis gives that to everyone. It is exactly like having your own personal assistant, doctor, project manager and finance advisor. Each one has a name, a voice and a personality, knows you and remembers what you told them, and you talk to them the way you would talk to a person: "Sara, I've had a headache since yesterday", "Maya, what does my day look like?". They answer out loud, ask each other for help when needed, and do the work for you.

The aim is that it feels real: not a chatbot you type into, but a team of people who are always there for you.

Meet your team

Maya

Personal assistant

Your schedule, email, reminders, daily plan, bills, forms and every personal task.

"Maya, what does my day look like?"

Ryan

Project manager and tech lead

Your projects, meetings, deadlines and coding work.

"Ryan, record this meeting and note my tasks."

Sara

Doctor and health coach

Your symptoms, lab reports, medicines, food, sleep and exercise.

"Sara, can you look at my blood test report?"

Victor

Finance advisor

Your budget, bills, savings, debt, investing basics and business numbers.

"Victor, help me make a monthly budget."

You can rename any of them and choose their voices. Jarvis itself is the front desk: if you don't say a name, it passes your request to the right person.

What your team can do

1Talk to them like real people

Press a shortcut in any app, speak, and the right member answers out loud in their own natural voice.

  • You stay with a member until you name someone else.
  • They first tell you what they will do and how long it takes ("On it, about 2 minutes"), then do it.
  • Your music pauses while you talk and resumes afterwards.
  • Speech recognition and voices run on your computer, so your voice never leaves it.

2They know you and remember you

Each member keeps their own notes about you and uses them in every answer, like a doctor with your file.

  • Sara keeps your health profile and every lab value; Victor your budget and goals; Ryan your projects; Maya your routine and writing style.
  • Each follows the method of their field, for example Sara checks warning signs first.
  • They ask each other for help when a question crosses fields.

3They work in your apps

Connect Gmail, Google Calendar, Microsoft 365, Notion, Slack, Spotify and more once, and your team can use them.

  • Read and sort your email, draft replies, check your calendar, play music.
  • You decide which member may use which app.
  • Reading is allowed, anything that sends or changes something asks you first, and deleting or paying always asks.

4Meetings and lectures

"Ryan, record this meeting": Jarvis records your microphone, the other people on the call and the screen, including every slide.

  • You get a transcript and minutes, and your tasks go on a board with reminders.
  • During a lecture or call, ask "what did he just say?" or "explain that".
  • In a recorded meeting, ask "what should I answer?" and the reply appears quietly on screen.

5Reminders and your daily plan

They speak up at the right time, like a real assistant would.

  • Reminders said out loud on time, in the member's own voice.
  • Maya plans your morning and wraps up your day in the evening.
  • Sara sets a reminder for every dose of a prescription, for exactly the right number of days.
  • Victor files your bills and has Maya remind you before they are due.

6Show them anything, and they show you

"Sara, I want to show you something": a drop box opens for a PDF, photo, video or recording, and she looks at it right away.

  • Videos are understood scene by scene, with a transcript of what is said.
  • When they make something for you, such as a plan, a report or a picture, it opens in a large preview next to the chat.

7Learn anything

Ask them to explain a topic and a narrated, step-by-step lesson plays beside your work, with diagrams, animated graphs and equations.

  • Explanations at your level: simple, student or expert.
  • They can look at your screen, a PDF or your code and explain exactly what is there.

8Projects and coding

"Ryan, add a login page to my project": the work runs in the background while you do something else.

  • Ryan brings you only the real decisions, with his recommendation, and gives spoken progress updates.
  • Risky steps, such as deleting files or publishing code, always wait for your OK.

9You stay in control

Your team can only do what you allow.

  • Every action is shown on the Activity page.
  • Your chats, notes and files stay on your computer.
  • Turn any tool or app on or off for each member at any time.

10Always within reach

A small voice card above the taskbar shows the answer; a floating chat box sits over your work. Both can be moved, resized and minimised, and everything you say there also appears in the main chat.
